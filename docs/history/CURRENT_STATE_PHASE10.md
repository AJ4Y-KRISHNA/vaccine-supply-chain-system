## Phase 9 verification record

- Simulator labels now originate from scenario injection state (`groundTruth.anomaly_active`, `groundTruth.anomaly_type`) rather than the 2–8 °C policy threshold. The label indicates the injected scenario event, not clinical or independently measured truth. ADR-013 documents this convention and the separation between authoritative policy and advisory AI.
- `backend/scripts/evaluateAiVsThreshold.js` completed 30 seeded runs per scenario (normal, door_open, compressor_drift, sensor_flatline, gps_jump), 400 measured samples plus 12 warm-up samples per run, and 10 separate normal-only held-out calibration runs. It saved `backend/results/ai_vs_threshold.json` and `.md`. The 61,800 evaluation samples contain 37,470 injected-anomaly-active and 24,330 normal samples.
- Detector calibration used training seed 90210 and separate held-out calibration seeds (`phase9-cal-normal-*`); evaluation used disjoint `phase9-eval-*` seeds. Held-out normal calibration FAR was 40/4,070 = 0.98% at cutoff 1.08380. Evaluation normal-sample FAR was 134/12,360 = 1.08%; 30/30 normal runs had at least one false alarm at this sample-level operating point. Overall AI precision/recall/F1 were 0.9551/0.9826/0.9687; threshold 1.0000/0.6234/0.7680; edge rules 0.7684/0.6422/0.6996. AI had 30/30 compressor-drift detections before the first threshold breach, averaging 234 seconds of lead. GPS-jump AI precision was only 0.4866 due to sparse positive samples and false sensor-feature activations. These are synthetic scenario-injection measurements, not real-world efficacy claims.
- Before replacement, the evaluator's “ground truth” was defined by a temperature excursion beyond 2–8 °C, making threshold metrics tautological. The old AI detector had no held-out calibration cutoff and primarily relied on temperature/humidity rolling median/MAD deviation (minimum five history samples); in the recorded old baseline of 266 samples it produced 72 positives, only 2 true positives (precision 0.0278, recall 0.0308, F1 0.0292). The revised detector fits normal-only features and calibrates on separate normal samples; its score remains advisory.
- Ethereal readiness now uses a 10-second probe timeout and 60-second cache. A slow SMTP probe is shown as degraded if recent capture is confirmed. Focused backend readiness/simulator tests passed 31/31; readiness frontend component test is included in a frontend run of 160 passing tests.

## Phase 10 verification record

- The backend Jest suite ran in `docker-compose.test.yml` on its own Compose network with ephemeral MongoDB replica-set storage (tmpfs), Redis, Mosquitto, Hardhat, and AI service. The test stack does not mount or join the live demo database/network, so no live worker or API was stopped and no live demo data was touched.
- During test setup, focused/manual end-to-end attempts also exposed a stale persisted-source expectation (`manual-override` instead of canonical `manual`) and test-only wait limits that were too short for local HTTP/socket delivery. The source assertions and bounded test waits/timeouts were corrected; those timeouts were environmental test-harness timing, not an external-worker code regression.
- First isolated Jest run completed with 57/59 suites and 347/349 tests passing. Both failures were stale assertions that hard-coded `127.0.0.1:8000` despite the isolated AI service being `http://ai-test:8000`; no external-worker regression or final environment timeout was observed. Assertions now honor `AI_BASE_URL`. The complete rerun passed 59/59 suites and 349/349 tests with `--runInBand --testTimeout=180000`; Jest reported 1040.846 seconds.
- AI-service test collection with the repository-root `.venv` was blocked because that environment lacks `statsmodels`; the AI-service-owned `ai-services/venv` includes `statsmodels 0.14.6`. Its first complete run found four stale single-sample anomaly expectations after introducing the 2-of-3 advisory persistence rule. Tests were revised to assert persistence behavior and a realistic multi-sample anomaly; the final full Pytest run passed 34 tests in 8.00 seconds.
- The final evaluator ran 30 seeded baseline runs for each of five scenarios, 400 measured samples plus 12 warm-up samples per run, and 17 robustness conditions with 30 runs each. It wrote `backend/results/ai_vs_threshold.json` and `backend/results/ai_vs_threshold.md` at 2026-10-07T04:08:21.854Z. Baseline total: 61,800 samples, of which 37,470 were injection-labelled active and 24,330 inactive. Normal-only scenario metrics requiring positive samples are `n/a`, not zero.
- Held-out normal calibration used 10 distinct original-setting runs: AI cutoff 1.068745, 40/4,070 alarms (0.983%); edge-comparator cutoff 1.796, 38/4,120 alarms (0.922%). The edge comparator is evaluation-only and uses 2-positive activation / 3-negative clear hysteresis; the actual approved-policy edge threshold path was not changed.
- In baseline normal runs, 2-of-3 AI persistence reduced runs with any false-alarm sample from 28/30 (raw) to 11/30, and false-alarm samples from 139/12,360 (1.125%) to 46/12,360 (0.372%). Mean first-detection delay over the 120 anomaly runs increased from 92.0 ± 120.0 seconds raw to 159.5 ± 125.1 seconds persisted. These are per-run means ± sample standard deviation.
- Compressor drift timing, on the same 30 seeds and measured from injected onset: threshold breach 1,998 ± 28 seconds; raw AI detection 114 ± 88 seconds (lead 1,884 ± 95 seconds); persisted AI detection 210 ± 90 seconds (lead 1,788 ± 94 seconds). The edge comparator first detected at 4,242 ± 28 seconds, after threshold breach, so it produced no pre-breach lead. “First threshold breach” is the first <2 or >8°C sample at/after injection; it is not the ground-truth label.
- The weakest baseline detector case was GPS jump: persisted AI F1 0.010 ± 0.020 and sample FAR 27.5% ± 17.2%; raw AI had recall 1.000 but precision 0.482. Robustness also showed severe cutoff sensitivity: at 10-second sampling, normal-run sample FAR was 78.5%; at noise ×2 it was 46.9%. These results are disclosed rather than presented as reliable operational detection.
- One preliminary evaluator execution wrote JSON but failed while formatting the Markdown prevalence table; the nested prevalence summary key was corrected. A subsequent full containerized pass hit one transient AI-service connection failure; the evaluator now retries transient network/5xx errors with bounded backoff. The final files above come from the successful rerun. A host-side attempt also timed out and was not used as evidence.

## Remaining limitations

1. All data, labels, locations, and metrics are synthetic; the local evaluation is not clinical evidence.
2. AI performance is measured against synthetic scenario-injection labels, not independently observed or clinical failures. Phase 10 persistence reduced baseline normal false alarms, but GPS-jump performance remains poor and noise/sampling perturbations cause high false-alarm rates. Approved policy rules remain authoritative.
3. Hardhat is a local development chain. A Compose volume reset redeploys a fresh local chain and removes previous chain history.
4. The evaluation uses 30 seeded local runs per baseline scenario and 30 per robustness condition, but remains a synthetic local experiment; metrics should not be generalized to production.
5. Visual testing across browsers and external email delivery were not executed in Phase 8.

## History

The earlier baseline text described the repository before its architecture-evolution phases. At that point the Edge Gateway had no durable spool; Redis Streams and consumer groups were absent; persistence, policy, and AI ran synchronously in the ingestion path; anchoring wrote individual raw records rather than Merkle windows; and dashboard updates relied mainly on polling. Those statements are retained here as historical context only and are superseded by the current-state table above.

The subsequent phases added reliable Compose services and synthetic seed data; correlation IDs and structured logs; deterministic simulation and the separate edge gateway; Redis Streams and consumer groups; regional forecasting and review-only supply plans; Merkle anchoring and per-reading QR verification; role-scoped live dashboard events; manual/CSV entry with append-only corrections; and the separate external worker. Phase 8 specifically added the independent worker/API restart proof, `MERKLE_WINDOW_SECONDS`, dependency conflict alignment, Admin route authorization coverage, clean-stack acceptance/evaluation, and an initial AI-versus-threshold comparison. Phase 9 replaced threshold-derived labels with simulator scenario-injection labels, introduced normal-only feature fitting and held-out cutoff calibration, and recorded measured AI/edge/threshold metrics.
